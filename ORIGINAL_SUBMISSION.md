## Idea
Our idea is to develop a unified smart tourism application for both domestic and international travelers visiting India. Users can securely register using verified credentials such as passport or visa details, ensuring authenticity and improving safety. The application provides a culturally enriched home screen that introduces visitors to Indian traditions, festivals, local customs, languages, and heritage, creating a more immersive travel experience. It also helps tourists discover nearby attractions, hidden gems, and recommended travel circuits through location-based suggestions. To enhance convenience, the platform integrates hotel bookings, cab services, hospital assistance, local guides, and digital payments, eliminating the need for multiple applications. A unique feature of the system is its automated safety mechanism, where travelers can perform periodic check-ins during their journey; if a check-in is missed for a prolonged duration, an alert can be generated for emergency response. In such situations, authorized agencies can access limited location information to help locate missing tourists while maintaining strict privacy protections. Additionally, anonymized tourist cluster analytics can assist authorities in managing crowds, improving infrastructure, and responding effectively during emergencies. By combining cultural engagement, seamless travel services, robust safety measures, and privacy-first principles, this platform aims to create a smarter, safer, and more connected tourism ecosystem across India.
